AEGIS P2P

Operational Guide & FAQ

1. Step-by-Step User Guide

1

Installation

AEGIS P2P is distributed outside standard app stores to guarantee your independence.

  • Download the .apk (Android) or .ipa (iOS) file from your customer portal.
  • On Android, open the file. If a "Play Protect" warning appears, tap "Install anyway". This proves the app is free from Google's control.
2

Creating access codes

No email, no phone number. You define your own keys:

  • Main PIN (6+ digits): Your real access key. Never forget it.
  • Decoy PIN: A second code. If forced to unlock, type this code. It opens a harmless fake dashboard.
  • Panic Code (0000/9999): Instantly wipes RAM and closes the app.
3

Adding a contact (3 methods)

AEGIS is decentralized (Peer-to-Peer). There is no central directory.

  • 1. Face-to-face: Go to "My QR Code". Your contact scans your screen using "Scan QR Code".
  • 2. Remote: Tap "Copy my key" and send this code to your contact via another channel. They will paste it into their app.
  • 3. Stealth Mode (Steganography): Copy your key, go to the Steganography module, hide the key in a poem, and send this poem via any channel (even unsecured). Your contact will paste it to extract the key.
4

Choosing your network (Transport Switch)

Adapt your communication mode to your tactical environment.

  • Tor: Maximum anonymity. Masks your IP address (slightly slower).
  • Wi-Fi Direct / BLE Mesh: Local communication without Internet (ideal if the network is down or jammed).
5

Sending a secure message

Once the contact is added, communication is end-to-end encrypted.

  • Go to the "Chat" tab or select your contact.
  • Write your message in the text area at the bottom.
  • Tap the send icon. The message is encrypted (Double Ratchet) instantly before leaving your device.
6

Sending a photo (Zero-Disk Ingest)

AEGIS never stores your media on the hard drive (ROM).

  • Go to the "Vault" or "Chat" tab.
  • Tap "Select Media" (or use the camera).
  • Choose your photo. AEGIS loads it directly into RAM.
  • The system automatically strips GPS metadata (EXIF) and applies Anti-PRNU camouflage.
  • Tap send. Once the app is closed, the photo exists nowhere.
7

Using Steganography

Hide keys in plain sight within harmless text.

  • Go to the "Steganography" tab.
  • Enter the key (or secret message) to hide, and write a poem or cover text.
  • Tap "Hide key in poem". The app generates text that looks normal but contains your secret.
  • Send this text via any network.
8

Emergency & Destruction (Kill Switch)

In case of physical compromise:

  • Access the emergency panel or type your panic code (0000/9999).
  • RAM is instantly purged, keys are destroyed, and the app closes. No trace remains.

2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is AEGIS P2P?

AEGIS P2P is a military-grade (Mil-Spec) communication suite. It is designed for armed forces, intelligence agencies, special operations operators, and critical infrastructure. It combines post-quantum encryption (ML-KEM-1024) with a strict "Zero-Disk" architecture.

2. Why isn't the app on Google Play or the App Store?

Tech giants impose rules (and censorship) that are incompatible with military security requirements. By distributing directly from our servers, we guarantee a secure Supply Chain, free from any interference or backdoor.

3. How does AEGIS resist state-level actors (APTs)?

AEGIS trusts no network infrastructure. By using native Tor routing and post-quantum encryption, even a state-level adversary capable of mass traffic interception will be unable to decrypt messages or identify operators.

4. Is the "Play Protect" warning on Android dangerous?

No. Android displays this warning for any app installed outside the Play Store. It is technical proof that the app escapes Google's control. You can install it safely.

5. What happens if I forget my Main PIN?

Due to our "Zero-Knowledge" architecture, no one — not even the AEGIS team — can recover your code. If you forget it, the operator and their data are permanently lost.

6. How does the Decoy PIN work?

If you are captured and forced to unlock the device, type the Decoy PIN. The app will open in a decoy environment (e.g., an empty notepad). Real OpSec communications remain completely invisible.

7. How do I use the Kill Switch (Destruction)?

In case of imminent physical compromise, typing the code (0000/9999) triggers an instant purge of RAM, destruction of session keys, and closure of the app. No memory dump can be exploited.

8. Does AEGIS collect metadata?

Zero. AEGIS collects no metadata, uses no trackers, and requires no identity. Operator identity is purely cryptographic and ephemeral.

9. What is "Zero-Disk" ingestion?

When an operator sends a photo or document, AEGIS never writes it to the device's storage (ROM). Everything is processed in RAM. If the device is seized or powered off, the data is gone forever.

10. How does the "Transport Switch" work?

It's a tactical network selector. The operator can force traffic via the Tor network (anonymity), Wi-Fi Direct (hardened local network), or Bluetooth Mesh (if Internet infrastructure is destroyed).

11. Can I use AEGIS without Internet (D3E zone)?

Yes. In a contested environment (jamming), AEGIS uses BLE Mesh or Wi-Fi Direct to establish a local mesh (MANET). Communications propagate from terminal to terminal.

12. What is Anti-PRNU protection?

It is a forensic counter-measure. AEGIS slightly alters the image to destroy the PRNU fingerprint (the unique "noise" of the camera sensor). It becomes impossible for a forensic lab to attribute a photo to a specific device.

13. Can I take screenshots?

No. For COMSEC reasons, AEGIS technically blocks screenshots (FLAG_SECURE) and hides the app preview in the task manager.

14. What happens if the device is seized or stolen?

The device contains no long-term data. If powered off or if the Kill Switch is activated, keys are wiped. An adversary will extract nothing, even via physical extraction or a Cold Boot attack.

15. Does AEGIS work on iOS?

Yes. For European operators, we distribute an iOS version directly via our website (Web Distribution - DMA), guaranteeing a supply chain outside of Apple's control.

16. Why does installation ask to allow "unknown sources"?

It's a requirement to bypass official stores. Manual installation guarantees the binary hasn't been modified. AEGIS is cryptographically signed by us.

17. How are updates secured?

Each update is signed via an ed25519 private key. If an adversary attempts a Man-in-the-Middle attack to push malicious firmware, the app will reject the update.

18. Is the source code auditable?

Yes. AEGIS's code undergoes reproducible builds and formal analysis (Miri, AWS Kani) with every commit. Security relies on mathematical proofs.

19. Is there a file size limit?

"Zero-Disk" processing in RAM imposes physical limits. Very large files must be compressed or fragmented before transmission to avoid saturating RAM.

20. What is "Post-Quantum" encryption?

AEGIS integrates ML-KEM-1024. It is an algorithm designed to resist attacks from a future quantum computer (capable of breaking RSA). Your communications today will remain undecipherable in 20 years.
